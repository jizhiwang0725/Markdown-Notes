# Intentions 
## Planned intentions
### Present simple with intentional verbs 
- **Intensional verbs**
  - *Plan to*
  - *Intend to*
  - *Hope to*
  - *Aim to*
  
  > *She **aims to** complete her degree early.*

### Present continuous
- **Fixed arrangement**  
  You have already taken steps to organize it, such as **setting a date, time, or buying a ticket.**  

  > *I **am flying** to Paris on Friday.*

### Be going to/Will
- **Informal**
  > *I am **going to** study tonight.*   

- **Formal**   
  > *The conference **will** start at 8 in the morning.*

## Unfulfilled past intention
### Past tense
- Talk about things that we **intended** to do, but **didn't.**  
  - *Consider*
  - *Expect to*
  - *Hope to*
  - *Think about*
  - *Want to*

  > *I **had hoped/hoped/was hoping/had been hoping** to visit the gallery before I left Florence, but it's closed on Mondays*  

  > *We **meant/were meaning** to call in and see you, but Marc wasn't feeling well.*

### Was/were going to
- Everyday conversation  

  > *I **was going to** call you, but I fell asleep.*

## Decision at moment of speaking 
### Will 
- Usually used as an **offer, request**
  
  > ***I'll** pick him up at eight.*

# Prediction
## Based on opinion or experience 
### Will
- Often used with:
  - *I bet (informal)*
  - *I reckon*
  - *I expect*
  - *I hope*
  - *I imagine*
  - *I wonder*
  - *I'm sure*
  
  > *Do you **reckon he'll** say yes?*

### Be going to
- Can also be used with those phrases, particularly in **informal contexts**
  
  > *I am expecting him to say yes*

## Based on present evidence 
### Be going to
- Prediction is based on **obvious evidences you just saw.** 
 
  > *The sky's gone really dark. **There's going to be** a storm.*
